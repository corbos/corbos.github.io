HOW TO PLAY

From scattered to sorted.

All 75 tiles are shuffled into five rows of fifteen. The five 1s are removed, shuffled, and placed in a fixed starting column, leaving five gaps.

Move a tile

Choose any numbered tile, then a highlighted gap. It must match the color of a neighbor and be exactly one higher than the tile on the left, or one lower than the tile on the right.

Shift a run

A consecutive same-color run ending in 15 can shift one space right if there is a gap immediately after it. Select any tile in an eligible run, then the gap. Easy requires at least 3 tiles, medium 4, and hard 5. You may select a shorter eligible suffix of a longer run.

Finish the puzzle

Arrange every row from its fixed 1 through 15. The last space of each completed row stays empty. No moves may go beyond the board. You lose if no actions remain, or if you get stuck repeating a loop. Undo is always available.
